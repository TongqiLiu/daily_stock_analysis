# Pattern Rules

## Image Terminology

- `TBs` = trapped buyers. These are buyers from prior highs who may sell into a rebound near breakeven, creating overhead supply.
- `BFs` = bottom fishers. These buyers enter near pullback lows and can help create higher lows if demand is real.
- `PT/LC` = profit taking / loss cutting. Early buyers take profit and weak holders cut losses during pullbacks; a valid base absorbs this supply with progressively less downside.
- A mature VCP shows fewer sellers on each pullback, tighter candles near the pivot, and volume drying up before demand pushes price through resistance.

## Setup Types

### VCP

Required traits:

- Prior advance of at least 20%-30% over the previous 1-3 months, or a clear post-base impulse.
- 2-4 contractions within the base.
- Each contraction is shallower than the prior one, for example 20% -> 12% -> 6%.
- Higher reaction lows or a rising lower trendline.
- Lower highs or a descending supply line until breakout.
- Volume declines during later contractions and expands on breakout.
- Pivot is the highest point of the final contraction or the descending supply line break.

Best quality:

- Final contraction depth is under 8%-10% for liquid large caps, or under 12%-15% for high-beta growth.
- Price is within 0%-5% of pivot before entry.
- Base is at least 3 weeks long but not so long that the trend has gone stale.

### Bull Flag

Required traits:

- Impulse leg advances at least 15%-25% in 1-4 weeks.
- Flag drifts sideways or downward for 5-25 trading days.
- Pullback gives back less than one third to one half of the impulse.
- Price holds the 10/21 EMA or reclaims them quickly.
- Volume contracts in the flag and expands on breakout.

Best quality:

- Flag forms above rising 50D moving average.
- Final candles become tight before breakout.
- Breakout closes near the daily high.

### Higher-Low Coil

Required traits:

- At least two meaningful reaction lows, each higher than the previous one.
- Resistance is flat or descending.
- Price compresses between rising support and overhead supply.
- Breakout trigger is above the short-term resistance line or prior-week high.

## Trend Template

Prefer these conditions:

- Current price above 50D and 200D moving averages.
- 50D moving average above or rising toward 200D.
- Price within 25% of a 52-week high or showing a strong new leadership turn.
- Relative strength vs QQQ/SPY is positive over 20-60 trading days.
- Average dollar volume is high enough for easy entry and exit.

Early-stage turnarounds can pass with a lower trend score if volume and relative strength are exceptional, but require smaller position size.

## Extension Rules

Use extension to avoid chasing:

- Ideal entry: within 0%-3% above pivot.
- Acceptable entry: within 5% above pivot if the stock is very liquid and the stop remains tight.
- Caution: more than 2-3 ATR above the 10/21 EMA.
- Avoid new entry: more than 4 ATR above the 10/21 EMA or more than 15%-20% above the 21 EMA for most liquid large caps.

For an existing position, extension is a reason to trail or trim, not an automatic sell.

## Volume Rules

- Base contraction: recent volume should be below the 20D average, ideally 50%-80% of average.
- Breakout confirmation: volume should be at least 1.4x 20D average, preferably 1.5x-2.0x.
- Failed breakout: breakout day volume is high but price closes back below pivot or inside the base.

## Entry Rules

- Standard breakout: buy as price clears pivot with volume confirming.
- Low-cheat: buy earlier when price reclaims 10/21 EMA or breaks a small internal downtrend line, only if stop can be placed near the final contraction low.
- Pullback entry: after breakout, buy first orderly pullback to 10/21 EMA only if pivot holds and volume dries up.

## Stop And Risk

- Default stop: below final contraction low or flag low.
- Volatility stop: 1-2 ATR below entry if structure stop is too tight/noisy.
- Maximum initial risk: generally 5%-8% for liquid large caps; 8%-12% for higher-beta growth only with smaller size.
- Account risk: 0.25%-0.50% for developing setups, 0.50%-1.00% for A-grade setups in a supportive market.

## Failure Conditions

Reject or downgrade when:

- Price breaks below the final higher low before triggering.
- Breakout occurs directly into earnings or a binary event.
- Breakout volume is absent and price reverses below pivot.
- The stock is over 4 ATR from the key moving average before entry.
- Broad market/sector is breaking down and the setup is not showing clear relative strength.
- The base has become too obvious, wide, or late-stage after multiple failed breakouts.

## Interpreting The User's Examples

- `ACLS`: VCP candidate if the third contraction holds higher lows and breaks the descending supply line with volume.
- `INTC`: fifth-week flag/higher-low coil; valid only if it clears the upper flag line or prior-week high and does not lose the latest higher low.
- `MCHP`: bull flag candidate; best if it breaks the flag line while holding the 10/21 EMA and volume expands.
- `ORCL`: triggered breakout example; after a 10%+ move, new entry is likely extension risk, while existing positions should manage by trailing/partial trim rather than chasing.
